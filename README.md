<h1>AETV privacy policy</h1>
<p class="meta">Developer: A.E studio · Effective 17 November 2026 · Applies to AETV (TV) and AETV mobile</p>
<h2>In short</h2>
<p>AETV is a media player. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer does not collect, receive or share any personal data.</p>
<h2>What stays on your device</h2>
<p>The playlist addresses and Xtream details you type in, your favorites, recently watched channels, viewing progress and saved copies of your lists are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or disconnecting a list in Settings, removes them.</p>
<h2>Network connections</h2>
<p>The app connects only to the servers you set up (your playlist or Xtream provider) and to the pictures and programme guides those servers point to. That provider sees your connection, as with any player, and its own privacy policy applies. The developer's servers are never involved.</p>
<h2>Setup page on the TV</h2>
<p>While the TV settings screen is open, the TV serves a small page on your home network so you can type your details from a phone or computer. What you type goes straight to the TV on your own network and nowhere else. The page stops when you leave the screen.</p>
<h2>No content</h2>
<p>AETV contains no channels, films or shows. You are responsible for having the right to use the content you connect.</p>
<h2>Children</h2>
<p>The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.</p>
<h2>Changes and contact</h2>
<p>If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: asafehud@gmail.com</p>
